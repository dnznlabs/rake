Rake Distro

Abstract

A distribution is a contract address that splits whatever it receives across a published table of beneficiaries, denominated in basis points. Anyone can trigger the split; only the owner can change the table, and every change waits out a public delay before it takes effect. The table, its labels, and any queued changes are readable in one call, so any interface — including the one serving this page — renders them without an indexer.

Motivation

Fees are suspicious by default: the paragraph explaining where they go can change silently. The incumbent answer grew into an on-chain banking product; integrators need the primitive underneath — one address, one table, one delay. Rake Distro is that primitive and nothing else.

How it works

Four verbs cover the lifecycle. receive — tokens accumulate at the address. distribute — anyone splits the balance by the table. queue — the owner stages a change in public. apply — anyone lands it after the delay.
